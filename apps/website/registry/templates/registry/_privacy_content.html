Participant privacy notice

This notice explains the participant information used to operate {{ site_full_title }} challenge platform. It must be reviewed and completed before public launch.

Who is responsible for your information

The data controller for {{ site_full_title }} challenge platform is {{ site_legal_name }} (company number {{ site_company_number }}).

Registered office: {{ site_registered_office }}.

Information we collect

  • Your participant nickname and email address.
  • Your securely hashed password and account-security records.
  • Registration, privacy-notice acknowledgement, verification, and account-status timestamps.
  • Your confirmation that you meet the challenge's 18+ participation rule, including the confirmation time and policy version. Your date of birth is processed only to make that eligibility check and is not stored.
  • Challenge run updates when that feature opens.
  • Administrative records needed to moderate entries and investigate abuse.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure participant accounts, reserve public nicknames, operate the challenge, moderate entries, publish approved results, and respond to participant requests.

Age eligibility

Participation is limited to people aged 18 or over. Before signup, the date you enter is used to calculate whether you meet that requirement and is then discarded. The account stores only the time of the successful eligibility check and the applicable policy version. This is not identity verification. If we reasonably believe that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may disable the account while we investigate and may remove it.

Our lawful bases

  • Contract: to create and operate the participant account and provide the requested challenge service, including essential account messages, run updates, and publication of the participant's nickname and approved results.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and administer the website, prevent abuse, moderate and validate entries, preserve challenge integrity, maintain essential technical logs, and anonymise information for aggregate statistics. We balance these interests against participants' rights and expectations.
  • Legal obligation: where information must be used or retained to meet an applicable legal requirement or respond to a data-rights request.

We do not currently rely on consent for core challenge operations. If we later introduce optional marketing or non-essential tracking, it will have a separate choice where required.

Public and private information

Your nickname and approved challenge results may be public. Your email address, password record, private account notes, and unapproved submissions are not displayed publicly.

Emails

Current emails are transactional messages required for verification, password recovery, or account security. The site does not currently send marketing mail or maintain a marketing subscription.

Service providers

The planned service providers are OVHcloud for hosting, Microsoft 365 for transactional email, and Cloudflare for connection security and human verification. The final production configuration will be recorded before launch.

Your choices

After logging in, you can download a copy of your participant data or request account closure and deletion. Requests are reviewed so challenge integrity, legal obligations, backups, and legitimate moderation records can be handled correctly.

Your UK data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erase personal information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • restrict how we use your information while an issue is considered;
  • provide applicable information in a portable format; or
  • consider an objection to processing based on legitimate interests.

These rights are not absolute and their application depends on the circumstances. We may need to verify your identity. We normally respond without undue delay and within one calendar month; if the law permits more time for a complex request, we will explain why.

How long we keep information

  • Unverified registrations are deleted after 30 days.
  • Active participant accounts are kept while the account and challenge platform remain active. We do not delete an account merely because it has not recently been used.
  • When an account closure is approved, its login credentials and identifying account information are removed.
  • Challenge reports and results may be retained indefinitely after being genuinely anonymised. Each retained run remains separate, its participant link is removed, and its public owner is displayed as {{ site_former_participant_label }}.
  • Moderation notes and evidence are kept until two years after the relevant run, decision, or account closure, unless an active dispute or legal obligation requires longer.
  • Routine security and email-delivery logs are kept for 90 days, unless they are needed for an active investigation.
  • Backups are retained on a rolling 30-day schedule. Deleted personal information disappears as those backups expire and is not restored to the live service.
  • Non-personal account-closure receipts containing only a random reference and timestamps may be kept indefinitely.

{{ site_legal_name }} reviews this schedule at least once a year and when the purpose of the platform changes.

Contact

Privacy and account-data enquiries can be sent to {{ site_privacy_email }}.

You may also contact us to object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will consider the circumstances and explain our response.

Complaints

If you believe we have handled your personal information incorrectly, please contact us so we have an opportunity to investigate and put things right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Visit ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or call its helpline on 0303 123 1113.
