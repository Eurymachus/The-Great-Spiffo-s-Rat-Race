Information we use

  • Your participant nickname, email address, securely hashed password and account-security records.
  • Registration, privacy-notice acknowledgement, email-verification, account-status and security timestamps.
  • Your confirmation that you meet the 18+ participation rule. Your date of birth is used only to perform that check and is not stored.
  • Challenge submissions and evidence, including game exports, character and run statistics, moderation history, uploaded images, evidence links and mod-review requests.
  • Messages, privacy requests, account-closure requests and records needed to respond to them.
  • Technical and security information such as IP address, browser or user-agent information, request times, security events and email-delivery results.
  • If you choose to connect Twitch, Discord or YouTube: the provider identity, public profile or channel information, granted permissions, encrypted access credentials and selected public or unlisted media needed for the requested connection.
  • Public Steam Workshop information returned when a mod is searched for or submitted for review.

We obtain most information directly from you or your Rat Race game export. Connected-account information comes from the provider you choose, and Workshop information comes from Steam.

Why we use it and our lawful bases

  • Contract: to create and operate your participant account, verify and recover access, process run updates, provide requested connected-account features, moderate submissions and publish your nickname and approved results.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and administer the service, prevent abuse, validate entries, investigate challenge-integrity concerns, maintain essential technical logs, preserve fair moderation records and create anonymised challenge statistics. We consider these uses necessary and proportionate to operating a secure and credible public challenge.
  • Legal obligation: where processing or retention is necessary to comply with applicable law or respond to a data-protection request.

Acknowledging this notice records that it was presented to you; it is not consent to core processing. We do not currently send marketing or use personal information for advertising.

What you must provide and what is optional

A nickname, email address, password and successful 18+ check are required to create an account. Without them, we cannot provide the participant service. Run submissions and supporting evidence are required only if you want a run reviewed. Twitch, Discord and YouTube connections, avatar uploads and mod-review requests are optional. There is no statutory requirement to provide this information.

What is public

Your nickname, approved challenge results, selected public evidence and authorised public moderation decisions may be displayed publicly. Your email address, password record, connected-account credentials, private notes and pending or declined submissions are not displayed publicly.

Service providers and sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the relevant feature:

  • OVHcloud provides server hosting.
  • Microsoft 365 delivers verification, password-recovery and account-security email.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS, secure tunnelling, traffic protection and Turnstile human verification.
  • Twitch, Discord and Google/YouTube process information when you choose to connect the corresponding account or select media.
  • Valve/Steam supplies public Workshop information used for mod search and review.
  • OpenAI may screen an uploaded avatar for safety when avatar moderation is enabled. The image is sent without your nickname or email address, and a human remains responsible for any moderation decision.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

International processing

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards, we rely on an applicable UK adequacy regulation or contractual safeguards used by the provider, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses. Contact us if you would like further information about the safeguard relevant to a particular provider.

Cookies and similar storage

The site uses only storage needed to provide and protect the service, including session, sign-in, security, CSRF-protection and Turnstile functions. We do not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural analytics or non-essential tracking. If that changes, we will update this notice and request a separate choice where required.

Automated checks

Turnstile and rate limits use automated signals to detect likely bots or abuse. When avatar moderation is enabled, an automated service may flag an image for review. These checks help protect the platform, but we do not use solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Challenge and account moderation decisions remain subject to human review.

How long we keep information

  • Unverified registrations are kept for no more than 30 days.
  • Active participant accounts are kept while the account and challenge platform remain active. We do not delete an account merely because it has not recently been used.
  • When an account closure is approved, login credentials and identifying account information are removed, subject to any information we must retain for an active dispute or legal obligation.
  • Challenge reports and results may be retained indefinitely after genuine anonymisation. Each retained run remains separate, its participant link is removed, and its public owner is displayed as {{ site_former_participant_label }}.
  • Moderation notes and evidence are kept until two years after the relevant run, decision or account closure, unless an active dispute or legal obligation requires longer.
  • Routine security and email-delivery logs are kept for up to 90 days, unless needed for an active investigation.
  • Backups are retained on a rolling 30-day schedule. Deleted personal information expires with those backups and is not intentionally restored to the live service.
  • Non-personal closure receipts containing only a random reference and timestamps may be kept indefinitely.

{{ site_legal_name }} reviews this schedule at least annually and whenever the platform's purpose or processing changes. Development and test information may be deleted sooner.

Your UK data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask us to give you access to your personal information; correct it; erase it where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it; restrict its use; provide applicable information in a portable format; or consider an objection.

Your right to object: you may object to processing based on our legitimate interests. We will consider your circumstances and stop the processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds to continue or it is needed for legal claims.

These rights are not absolute. We may need to verify your identity, and we will explain any lawful limitation that applies. We normally respond without undue delay and within one calendar month; if the law permits more time for a complex request, we will explain why.

After signing in, you can download a copy of your participant data or request account closure. You can disconnect an optional provider from account settings, although records already required for moderation or challenge integrity may still be retained under the schedule above.

Changes to this notice

We will keep this notice under review. If we plan to use personal information for a materially different purpose, we will update the notice and bring the change to participants' attention before that processing begins where required.
