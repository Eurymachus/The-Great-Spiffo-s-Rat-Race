Participant privacy notice

This notice explains the participant information used to operate The Great Spiffo's Rat Race challenge platform. It must be reviewed and completed before public launch.

Who is responsible for your information

The data controller for The Great Spiffo's Rat Race challenge platform is Sentinel Tech Ltd.

Information we collect

  • Your participant nickname and email address.
  • Your securely hashed password and account-security records.
  • Registration, consent, verification, and account-status timestamps.
  • Challenge run updates when that feature opens.
  • Administrative records needed to moderate entries and investigate abuse.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure participant accounts, reserve public nicknames, operate the challenge, moderate entries, publish approved results, and respond to participant requests.

Public and private information

Your nickname and approved challenge results may be public. Your email address, password record, private account notes, and unapproved submissions are not displayed publicly.

Emails

Current emails are transactional messages required for verification, password recovery, or account security. The site does not currently send marketing mail or maintain a marketing subscription.

Service providers

The planned service providers are OVHcloud for hosting, Microsoft 365 for transactional email, and Cloudflare for connection security and human verification. The final production configuration will be recorded before launch.

Your choices

After logging in, you can download a copy of your participant data or request account closure and deletion. Requests are reviewed so challenge integrity, legal obligations, backups, and legitimate moderation records can be handled correctly.

How long we keep information

  • Unverified registrations are deleted after 30 days.
  • Active participant accounts are kept while the account and challenge platform remain active. We do not delete an account merely because it has not recently been used.
  • When an account closure is approved, its login credentials and identifying account information are removed.
  • Challenge reports and results may be retained indefinitely after being genuinely anonymised beneath the shared Redacted participant.
  • Moderation notes and evidence are kept until two years after the relevant run, decision, or account closure, unless an active dispute or legal obligation requires longer.
  • Routine security and email-delivery logs are kept for 90 days, unless they are needed for an active investigation.
  • Backups are retained on a rolling 30-day schedule. Deleted personal information disappears as those backups expire and is not restored to the live service.
  • Non-personal account-closure receipts containing only a random reference and timestamps may be kept indefinitely.

Sentinel Tech Ltd reviews this schedule at least once a year and when the purpose of the platform changes.

Contact

Privacy and account-data enquiries can be sent to thegreatspiffo@machus.co.uk.

The final lawful bases and jurisdiction-specific wording are still pending. Do not treat this draft as the final public legal notice.
