# Lawful Bases and Legitimate Interests Assessment

Approved by the project owner on 2026-07-15 for Sentinel Tech Ltd's operation
of The Great Spiffo's Rat Race challenge platform. Review this document before
public launch and whenever a processing purpose materially changes.

## Lawful-basis map

| Processing | Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration, verification, login, account recovery, essential account email, run updates, and publication of nickname and approved results | Provide the participant-requested challenge service | Contract — UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b) |

Acknowledging the privacy notice records that it was presented to a participant;
it is not consent to core processing.

## Legitimate Interests Assessment

### Purpose test

Sentinel Tech Ltd has legitimate interests in keeping the service secure,
preventing abuse, running fair moderation, validating submissions, preserving
challenge integrity, and retaining useful statistics without unnecessary
identification. Participants and the wider Rat Race community also benefit from
secure accounts, trustworthy results, and fair competition.

### Necessity test

The processing is limited to information and access reasonably needed for those
purposes. Security checks and short-lived logs help detect abuse; restricted
moderation records provide evidence for decisions; validation protects the
leaderboard; and anonymisation permits historical statistics without retaining
the closed participant's identity. These purposes cannot reasonably be achieved
with no processing, while less intrusive aggregate or anonymised information is
used whenever identification is no longer needed.

### Balancing test

Participants reasonably expect account security, human verification, report
validation, moderation, and action against cheating or abuse in an organised
public challenge. The platform does not intentionally collect special-category
or criminal-offence data. Email addresses and unapproved submissions remain
private; passwords are hashed; staff access is role-scoped; retention periods
are finite; and participants can download their account data, request closure,
or object through the published privacy contact. On that basis, the limited
privacy impact does not override the identified interests.

### Outcome and review triggers

Legitimate interests is considered proportionate for the activities above with
the stated safeguards. Reassess before introducing behavioural analytics,
marketing, new categories of personal information, automated decision-making,
processing aimed at children, or materially different report/moderation uses.
