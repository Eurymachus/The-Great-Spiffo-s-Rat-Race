# MOD Decision 003: Preserve the Official Challenge Contract

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-16

## Context

Tracker work began from the official Rat Race rules and map. The requested tooling must report and verify the existing challenge, not redesign it.

## Decision

Preserve the official contract:

- Ironman: one character and one world per attempt; retries are separate attempts.
- Any traits and occupations are allowed.
- Avoid exploits and use approved settings/mods.
- Debug intervention is limited to provable lethal non-player-error bugs.
- Complete all outposts, reach level 10 in every accepted player skill, and reach 1,000,000 character-info zombie kills.
- Full-run streaming is required for bounty eligibility; offline participation is not bounty-eligible.

## Consequences

- Tracker features report progress and evidence; they do not modify the challenge.
- Respawn remains unavailable in Rat Race variants.
- Skill-set enumeration, submission messaging, and exact end-to-end win certification still require implementation decisions.
