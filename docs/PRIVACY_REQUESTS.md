# Privacy Requests and Complaints

Sentinel Tech Ltd receives privacy and account-data requests at
`support@tgsrr.com`.

## Operator checklist

1. Record the date received and the requested right or complaint.
2. Verify identity proportionately before disclosing or changing account data.
3. Locate relevant live, administrative, email-delivery, and retained records.
4. Consider whether the requested right applies and whether a lawful limitation
   requires any information to be retained.
5. Respond without undue delay and normally within one calendar month. If a
   lawful extension is needed for complexity, tell the participant and explain
   why within the initial period.
6. Record the decision and actions without retaining unnecessary copies of
   identity evidence.
7. Explain the participant's right to complain to the Information Commissioner's
   Office if they are dissatisfied.

Participants can download their current account data and request closure from
their account page. Other rights, objections, and complaints are handled through
the published privacy contact until dedicated workflows are introduced.

ICO complaint information: `https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/`.
